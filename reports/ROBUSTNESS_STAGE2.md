Generated by `scripts/94_robustness_report.py` from `results/a2/robustness/`. ## What the paper can claim (section 3a wording)

**I1 fails.** The G-Signal-2 pass is reported as recorded, together with the statement that a trained-shuffled control matches it. No gene-conditioning claim is made, and the paper is a negative result.

**I3 fails** (I1 fails; with the siRNA token zeroed the gain over agnostic keeps 71% of its size (p = 1e-05), so the R3(a) part would hold); the gain is not shown to be gene-level rather than reagent-level, and the screenB result (independent siRNAs, token masked) is the explanation.


Fixed wording (section 4), whatever R1–R4 show:

- **G-ZeroShot-2:** "passes the pre-registered rule on test for one of five sources (string) with a CI bound at −0.0001; the rule has no correction across sources; the same source fails on validation. We do not claim zero-shot transfer."
- **G-Plan-2:** "fails on validation and test; planning is not distinguishable from kNN-imputed retrieval."
- **ScreenB:** reported in the main text, not only in the supplement.
